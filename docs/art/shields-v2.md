# FamBit shield artwork — silhouette revision v2

The v1 collection looked too similar at dashboard scale. This revision gives every tier a distinct silhouette and dominant color.

| Tier | Silhouette | Primary material/color |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Bronze | Round buckler | Brown oak with a bronze rim |
| Silver | Slender elongated kite | Cool silver and cobalt blue |
| Gold | Broad heater | Rich yellow gold |
| Epic | Angular crystal hexagon | Violet and amethyst |
| Legendary | Wide winged star crest with crown | Ivory wings, crimson core, gold trim |

Production assets: `public/shields/aaa-v2/{tier}-{256,512}.webp`.
Generation: built-in image generation tool, transparent alpha, using [the original sheet](./shields-concept-v1.png) as a rendering-style reference only.
The dashboard showcase uses 48px emblems; the compact stats strip remains 30px. Morning/evening/bonus marks are separate overlays.

## Shared generation prompt

Create ONE standalone premium fantasy RPG inventory shield asset for FamBit. The attached old collection is a MATERIAL/RENDERING STYLE REFERENCE ONLY. The old collection FAILED at small sizes because every shield had almost the same narrow pointed shape. You MUST REPLACE the target shield's geometry with the NEW distinctly recognizable silhouette specified below. Preserve the sophisticated AAA sculpted craftsmanship and physical metal lighting quality, but NOT the old repeated geometry. This is an intentional redesign for color AND silhouette recognition at 30–44 pixels. Large clean masses, bold unmistakable outside contour, strongly differentiated dominant color, restrained secondary engraving, no tiny filigree noise. Symmetrical front-facing view, sculpted three-dimensional relief, no perspective distortion, consistent warm studio key upper-left and cool fill. One isolated shield, centered on a square 1024 canvas, visible shape fills 88–92% of the canvas in its longest dimension, complete silhouette with safe margin. REAL TRANSPARENT ALPHA background; no opaque black/gray, no fake checkerboard, no backdrop or ground shadow. NO TEXT, labels, numbers, logos, watermarks, frames or UI. No hands, characters or weapons. The five-tier family uses: circular wood bronze, slender cool silver kite, broad gold heater, angular violet crystal hexagon, and broad ivory-winged crimson legendary star crest. Make only the one specified target.

## Tier-specific prompts

### bronze

BRONZE TARGET. A perfectly ROUND WOODEN SHIELD, unmistakably circular, diameter 1:1. Thick aged honey-brown oak planks with wide readable grain, rugged dark bronze/iron circular rim, six or eight widely spaced bronze rivets, a plain large forged dark-bronze round central boss. A pair of restrained bronze reinforcing bands integrated across the wooden planks. Warm earthy brown dominates at least 65% of the visible area, dark bronze rim 25%, boss 10%. Weathered utilitarian dignity, tactile real wood, forged metal, subtle wear. No pointed lower tip, no kite shape, no crown, wings, gem, magic or ornate golden face. The lowest rarity must feel like a humble well-made round wooden buckler, instantly different from every metal tier. Completely circular outer silhouette.

### silver

SILVER TARGET. An exceptionally SLENDER ELONGATED KITE shield with a simple sharp bottom point, straight angled shoulders, softly arched top, long clean smooth sides, overall visible width about 57% of height. Dominate with COOL BRIGHT SILVER and pale icy blue metal, NOT gold. Thick polished silver edge, a large very readable vertical cobalt-blue inset/spine filling the center, ONE modest icy-blue diamond gem. Noble restrained shallow engravings, clean broad metallic facets, no gold filigree. This is the only long narrow unwinged silhouette in the set. No side spikes or broad ornaments. Clarity at 30px is paramount: mostly bright silver outline surrounding strong blue central stripe.

### gold

GOLD TARGET. A BROAD CLASSIC HEATER shield, width about 85% of height, with a near-flat gently arched top edge, two broad squared upper shoulders, straight upper sides that converge into ONE broad bottom point. NO tall top spike, no wings, no crown. Its visual mass is much broader and squatter than Silver. Saturated rich GOLD and warm golden yellow dominate 75% of face, bold golden embossed sun/lion-like heraldic medallion centered with subtle amber inset; simple symmetric regal sculpted relief and broad polished chamfered gold rim. Dark recessed engraving only accents, not a large dark face. Prestigious, obviously regal, but not divine. At 30px it reads as a broad solid GOLD HEATER, immediately distinct from Silver's thin silver-blue kite, Epic's purple hexagon and Legendary's white-winged red star crest.

### epic

EPIC TARGET. A strongly ANGULAR, NEAR-HEXAGONAL CRYSTAL SHIELD, width about 90% of height. A short top point, two pronounced wide sharp lateral points at the mid-shoulders, lower angled facets and a compact bottom point create an unmistakable wide geometric diamond/hexagonal outline. Its area is dominated by deep saturated VIOLET/AMETHYST, not gold. Large faceted purple crystalline armor panels edged in dark gunmetal and a few cool silver bevels. One bold luminous magenta-violet hexagonal central crystal occupies 35% of the face, broad vivid purple channels, restrained arcane engraving. A few deliberate angular purple crystal projections are part of the silhouette. Enchanted powerful and premium. NO gold wing shapes, NO rounded heater geometry, NO feather wings or crown. Its compact, jagged violet crystal silhouette must be recognizable even rendered 30px.

### legendary

LEGENDARY TARGET, COMPLETE SILHOUETTE REDESIGN. An extraordinary DIVINE WINGED STAR-CREST SHIELD, approximately square footprint, visibly wider than Gold: two large sculpted IVORY-WHITE / luminous PLATINUM celestial wings flare UP AND OUT symmetrically with three bold feather-blade lobes per side. A tall THREE-POINTED GOLD CROWN is integrated above the central shield. The central shield is a large RUBY-CRIMSON faceted shield-shaped heart/core, almost half the full width and 65% of full height, with a very bright distinct RED ruby center and dark red crystal facets. A restrained gilded eight-point star frame surrounds the red heart. A sharp central bottom star point completes the silhouette. From 30px this must read as WHITE WINGS + RED CORE + THREE-POINT CROWN, not as another yellow gold pointed shield. Ivory-white wings and deep ruby/crimson face together form at least 70% of visible pixels; radiant gold is narrow edgework and crown, not the main area. Huge prestige and divine craftsmanship, controlled small golden-white light hugging edges, ruby inner radiance, tiny flame-like carved tips, NO diffuse aura clouds or particle noise. Dramatic broad winged silhouette and confident simple masses, exceptionally precious materials. Make the wing architecture so strong that its solid silhouette alone could never be mistaken for Gold's plain broad heater. This must be the unmistakable apex rarity, beautiful and powerful at 30–44px.
